      How a round works

      1. Seal. You post hash(move ‖ secret); your move stays hidden.
      2. Reveal. Once both seals are final, both moves are revealed and checked against the seals.
      3. Dice. BLAKE2b of both secrets rolls the die; a 6 is a critical hit.
      4. Deadlines. No seal within 30 s, or no reveal within 15 s of block time, forfeits the round.
